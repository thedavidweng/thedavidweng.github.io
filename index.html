03 — How I work

Three rules I don’t break.

i.

Local-first

Your data and your models stay on your machine. On-device inference, encrypted storage, offline compilers — the network is optional, never required.

ii.

Agent-native

Stable contracts, predictable exit codes, dry-runs, and explicit gates. If an AI agent can drive a tool safely, a human can too.

iii.

Designed, not decorated

A background in graphic design and video production taught me that restraint is a feature. Every pixel earns its place — this page included.
